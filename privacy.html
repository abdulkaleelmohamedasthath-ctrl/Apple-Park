Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

APPLE PARK respects the privacy of customers who browse our website or contact us about orders.

Information you provide

When you prepare a WhatsApp order, you may enter your name, phone number, delivery address, order notes and selected products. These details are used to prepare your order message. Sending the message through WhatsApp is your choice.

How information is used

We use details you send to answer enquiries, confirm prices and availability, arrange orders and delivery, and provide customer support.

WhatsApp and external services

When you open WhatsApp or Google Maps, those services process information under their own privacy policies. Our website may also use browser storage to remember shopping-bag selections and site preferences.

Data retention and security

Messages sent to the store may be retained for order handling, customer service and applicable record-keeping needs. No online transmission is completely secure. Please do not send bank passwords, card PINs or other sensitive credentials.

Your choices

You can browse without submitting an order. Contact us to ask about customer information you have sent, including requests to correct or delete it where applicable.

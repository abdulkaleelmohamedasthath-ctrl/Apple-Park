Please contact APPLE PARK before returning a product. This page explains how to request help; it does not promise a specific return period or automatic refund.

Confirm the product model, condition, compatibility, warranty coverage, and any product-specific return conditions with the store.

If there is a problem

Eligibility for repair, replacement or refund depends on the product, the circumstances, applicable warranty terms and consumer protection law. The store will discuss available options after reviewing your request.
